Bases: BaseModel

One feature (plane or cylinder) of a part: PARTS[part][surface].

The keys are those of the dictionary (see docs/guide/input_format.md). Keys that are not listed (e.g. META, CAD_ID) are kept and ignored. Which keys are required depends on the other keys and on the interactions; those checks need the part and surface labels and are made by SystemData.

  • otaf.exceptions.MissingKeyError – If a required key is missing (MissingSurfaceTypeKeyError for TYPE).

  • otaf.exceptions.InvalidPartLabelError, otaf.exceptions.InvalidSurfaceLabelError – For malformed part or surface labels.

  • otaf.exceptions.UnsupportedSurfaceTypeError – If TYPE is not plane or cylinder.

  • otaf.exceptions.InconsistentContactConstraintsError – If the two surfaces of a contact have different CONSTRAINTS_G.

  • NotImplementedError – For a contact between a plane and a cylinder.

  • otaf.exceptions.SystemDataValidationError – For any other problem; lists all of them.
